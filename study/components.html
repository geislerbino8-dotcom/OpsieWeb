Four production patterns extracted from the Opsie codebase, styled with the conventions from items 1–2. Study each block: the cite tells you where the real version lives, the snippet shows the exact classes, the notes say why. Then do the checklist at the bottom without looking.

1 · Buttons

real: HeroPage.tsx:111 · Navigation.tsx (Get Started) · TaskBoard add-btn pattern

House rule: every button is a pill. White CTAs stay white — they are the accent, not the violet. Hover for primary flips to white/black (that contrast pop is the site's signature).

class="rounded-full bg-[#8B5CF6] px-8 py-3 text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.2em]
       text-white transition-all duration-300 hover:bg-white hover:text-black"

Why: radius comes from utilities (site-wide pill rule). Disabled = opacity and cursor-not-allowed — never kill only one of them. Focus any button: the violet *:focus-visible outline appears (index.css:332).

2 · Cards

real: ContactForm.tsx:68 · MapCard.tsx:12 · WhyChooseUs.tsx:36 · glow: index.css:39

The card recipe is four rules, always together: carbon surface + violet hairline border + .card-side-glow + transition-all duration-500 with a lift. Hover both cards to feel the difference.

before — generic

Plain card

Neutral surface, no identity, no glow, hover does almost nothing. Correct markup — wrong skin.

after — Opsie recipe

Styled card

bg-[#0a0a0a] + border-[#8B5CF6]/25 + glow + hover:-translate-y-1. The glow bleeds from the left & right edges only (x-offsets ±24px do the work).

class="card-side-glow rounded-2xl border border-[#8B5CF6]/25 bg-[#0a0a0a] p-6
       transition-all duration-500 hover:-translate-y-1 hover:border-[#8B5CF6]/60"

/* index.css — unlayered so utilities can't override it */
.card-side-glow { box-shadow: -24px 0 48px -20px rgba(139,92,246,.6),
                               24px 0 48px -20px rgba(139,92,246,.6); }

4 · Inputs

real: ContactForm.tsx:93 (all four fields + textarea)

Exact field recipe from the contact form. Focus = violet ring (no layout shift: ring, not border-width). Error is the only place a non-palette color is allowed — validation only.

class="w-full rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.04] px-4 py-3
       transition-all duration-300 placeholder:text-gray-400
       focus:outline-none focus:ring-2 focus:ring-[#8B5CF6]"
